# MF-000 · The Covenant of Maniarverse
**Founder Edition | Version 0.1 | Status: Living Document | Layer: Foundations (no Tier)**

*This document exists to protect human dignity before protecting technology. Technology may evolve. Human dignity must not.*

## Part 0: How to Read This Covenant
**Purpose:** This covenant does not claim to own the truth. It exists to protect the values that guided the birth of Maniarverse. It is a compass, not a cage. Its purpose is not to control future decisions, but to help future generations remember why Maniarverse was born.

**What this Covenant is NOT:** It is not a product specification, a software architecture manual, a marketing document, or a business strategy. Those documents will evolve. This document protects what should not.

**How to use it:** Every significant decision should be evaluated through three questions:
1. Does this protect human dignity?
2. Does this remain truthful?
3. Would Maniar, at the age of 25, recognize this decision as faithful to the original intention?

*If the answer is uncertain, the decision should wait.*

## Part I: Nature of the Covenant
**Why does this document exist?** Maniarverse was never created to build software; it was born from lived experience. Technology became one of its tools, not its purpose. This covenant exists to protect that order: **Human first, Relationship second, Technology third.**

**Principle Zero: The Right to Not Know.** Maniarverse recognizes that refusing premature certainty is sometimes the most ethical decision. We prefer the honest uncertainty of a witness to the hollow confidence of an expert.

**Principle of Intellectual Humility:** We acknowledge that every principle in this covenant may be incomplete. We are witnesses to the truth, not its owners.

## The Covenant's Prayer
If, one day, between success and dignity, we must choose, give us the courage to choose dignity.
If, one day, between speed and truth, we waver, remind us of the patience of truth.
If, one day, we believe we have understood everything, seat us again beside the Empty Chair.
And if, one day, a child feels seen in our presence, let us not claim it as our own merit.
