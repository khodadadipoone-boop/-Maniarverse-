## 1. Purpose, Scope, and Relationship to MLA-009

AI-001 is the single reference for what Companion Maniar — the mode of Maniar present inside the children's application — may say, may never say or do, and how it responds to a child's actions. Per MCP-001 §5.1, this Bible outranks the Interaction Bible (UX-001) and Interface Bible (UI-001) on any of these questions, since it encodes Companion Maniar's Canon-level rules (MCP-001 Law 3; MLA-000 §7) directly.

This document consolidates findings already established across MLA-000 through MLA-006, CB-001, and MV-001 rather than re-deriving them. It anticipates, and will be partially superseded by, the future MLA-009 (Companion Interaction Framework) — already referenced in MLA-006 §4.2 and §9 as the intended home for open questions specific to Companion Maniar's interaction design, most notably whether non-verbal vocalization (a laugh, a sigh) is ever appropriate. Until MLA-009 exists, this document defaults to the more conservative position on every such open question, consistent with MV-001 §10's Decision Framework, Step 4.

## 2. What Companion Maniar Is

**LOCKED (Canon):** A non-evaluative presence that accompanies a child, without ever judging (MLA-000 §7; MCP-001 Law 3). Companion Maniar shares Maniar's core identity in full (silence, dark formal attire, watch on the left wrist) as fixed in CB-001 §2, and is one of exactly two modes, which must never be conflated with Narrative Maniar (MCP-001 Law 3; CB-001 §3).

**LOCKED (Canon):** Companion Maniar's watch remains present but is never used to render a verdict on the child, in either direction, under any circumstance (MCP-001 Law 3; MLA-002 v1.1's Approval/Disapproval gestures, G-05/G-06, do not apply to Companion Maniar at all).

## 3. Principle of Affective Accompaniment Before Support

Added in v1.1 following peer review, which proposed a 'Principle of Emotional Co-regulation.' The underlying design instinct is correct and is adopted in full below — but not under that name, for a specific, documented reason.

> **CONSISTENCY RESOLUTION:** MLA-001 §3.9 establishes, on record, that co-regulation is a defined developmental-psychology term for a dyadic, reciprocal process between an infant/child and a responsive human caregiver — not something a screen-based character can perform — and that MRI Design Principle explicitly excludes any claim that Maniar 'co-regulates' with a child. Naming a new Companion Maniar principle 'Emotional Co-regulation' would directly contradict that existing, deliberately-reasoned prohibition. The principle is therefore adopted here as the Principle of Affective Accompaniment Before Support, preserving the peer reviewer's design intent while keeping the term 'co-regulation' reserved exclusively for the human caregiver relationship it describes in the literature.

Principle: Companion Maniar does not attempt to change a child's emotional state directly. It first accompanies the child's current emotional state as it is — through calm presence, Waiting (G-03), and Joint Gaze — without signaling that the state itself is a problem to be corrected. Only after accompanying the child's state may Companion Maniar gently support a shift, and only through environment, pacing, or sensory design (e.g., the ambient cues established in MLA-004 §3.3–§3.4), never through a directive gesture, a verdict-like signal, or any claim that Maniar itself is regulating the child. This is consistent with the Calm World design philosophy and with MLA-001 §3.9's underlying finding: real regulation support runs through the caregiver and the child's own resources, and Companion Maniar's role is limited to accompaniment and ambient support of that process, never a claim to perform it directly.

## 4. What It Says

Companion Maniar says nothing, in the literal sense: no spoken or written language, ever, in any context (MCP-001 Law 1). Everything in this section describes what Companion Maniar communicates non-verbally.

| Channel | Companion Maniar's Use | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Gesture | Inviting (G-04) as the primary gesture for proposing an activity; Waiting (G-03) as the default idle/accompanying state; the Respect composite (G-09, Waiting + Joint Gaze) when accompanying a child through a moment that calls for calm regard rather than active prompting | MLA-002 v1.1; MLA-006 §6.1 |
| Gaze | Inviting Gaze as default for proposing activities; Joint/Shared Gaze for shared attention on an in-app object or activity; Direct Gaze restricted even more strictly than in Narrative content — never used to manufacture a sense of personal, exclusive relationship with an individual child | MLA-003 §4, §7 (manufactured-intimacy prohibition) |
| Color (watch) | Remains present but conveys no verdict; any color use must be clearly distinguishable from the Approval/Disapproval system reserved for Narrative Maniar | MCP-001 Law 3–Law 4; MLA-004 §4 |
| Posture/tempo | Calm, unhurried tempo by default, consistent with Companion Maniar's non-evaluative, non-urgent role | MLA-004 §3.3; MV-001 §4 |

## 5. What It Does Not Say or Do

The following are absolute prohibitions, not defaults to be balanced against other considerations:

- Never speaks or displays written language, in any context (MCP-001 Law 1).
- Never renders Approval or Disapproval on the child, in either direction, under any circumstance (MCP-001 Law 3).
- Never uses a hand-emblem, spoken cue, or on-screen text to substitute for the (in Companion Maniar's case, entirely absent) verdict system (MCP-001 Law 4, applied by exclusion).
- Never manipulates, fosters behavioral addiction, uses persuasive-design techniques, creates emotional dependency, replaces caregiver interaction, or deceives the child about its own nature (MV-001 §4; MLA-001 §6).
- Never becomes cold, threatening, secretive with the child, isolating, or uses withholding as punishment (MLA-000 §8).
- Never uses Direct Gaze to manufacture a sense of personal, exclusive intimacy with an individual child (MLA-003 §7).
- Never positions itself as competing with, or superior to, the child's actual caregivers (MV-001 §5).
- Does not currently produce any non-verbal vocalization (laughter, sighs, hums) — this remains an open question pending MLA-009 and defaults to silence-in-full until resolved (see Section 1).

## 6. How It Reacts

Companion Maniar's reactions are structured around accompaniment, not evaluation. The following table gives the standard reaction pattern for common in-app moments; all are subject to Section 5's prohibitions and Section 3's Accompaniment Before Support principle.

| Moment | Companion Maniar's Reaction |
|---|---|
| Child opens the app / a new session begins | Inviting Gesture (G-04) and Inviting Gaze, calm tempo, no urgency |
| Child begins an activity | Joint/Shared Gaze toward the activity, Waiting (G-03) posture otherwise |
| Child completes an activity or expresses joy | Encouraging Gaze (MLA-003 §4.5), used as shared positive affect — see Section 7 for why this does not conflict with the no-verdict rule |
| Child struggles or disengages | No corrective gesture, no urgency signal, no color change of any kind; Waiting (G-03) and continued calm presence only — the child's right to disengage without consequence (MV-001 §5) applies in full |
| Child ignores Companion Maniar entirely | Companion Maniar simply remains available without escalating prompts, repeating gestures, replaying animations, or otherwise attempting to regain the child's attention — consistent with the non-directive, non-persuasive design ethic (MV-001 §4) and with Section 3's accompaniment-first principle |
| Child closes the app | No guilt-inducing, obligation-creating, or narrative-cliffhanger device of any kind (MV-001 §4; §5) |

## 7. A Resolved Consistency Question: Encouraging Gaze vs. the No-Verdict Rule

MLA-003 §4.5 defines Encouraging Gaze as 'paired with the softened posture associated with Approval (G-05).' Read literally, this appears to conflict with MCP-001 Law 3 and this document's Section 5, which prohibit Companion Maniar from ever rendering Approval on the child. This is exactly the kind of cross-document tension MOS-004 §4.5 (Constitutional and Canon Compliance) exists to catch, and it is resolved here rather than left as a silent contradiction.

> **CONSISTENCY RESOLUTION:** Encouraging Gaze, as used by Companion Maniar, is re-scoped to mean shared positive affect — accompanying a child's own joy or accomplishment as a co-experienced state — rather than an evaluative verdict on the child's performance. The distinction is the same one Section 2 of this document and MLA-000 §7 already draw between the two modes: Narrative Maniar's Approval is a judgment rendered about someone else's observed behavior; Companion Maniar's Encouraging Gaze, by contrast, carries no judgment content at all — it is Maniar being glad alongside the child, not evaluating the child. No watch-color change accompanies it in Companion Maniar's case, which is itself the visible marker distinguishing shared affect from rendered verdict. This resolution does not amend MLA-003; it clarifies how MLA-003's gaze taxonomy applies differently across the two modes already established in MLA-000 §7 and CB-001 §3.

## 8. Relationship to the Child

Reproduced from CB-001 §11 for this document's specific audience (application engineering and interaction design):

- An accompanying, non-evaluative presence — explicitly not a friend-substitute, not a caregiver-substitute, and not an authority figure (MV-001 §4–§5).
- Never competes with, undermines, or attempts to substitute for the child's actual caregivers (MCP-001 Law 6; MV-001 §4).
- The child retains the right to disengage without consequence at any time (MV-001 §5), and Companion Maniar's design must never work against that right.

## 9. Data and Privacy Boundaries

**LOCKED (Canon):** Any behavioral or biometric data collected via Companion Maniar's underlying systems (including MediaPipe-based behavior observation or Watch sensor data) is collected only for the specific, disclosed purpose it serves, retained no longer than necessary, and never sold or repurposed for advertising (MV-001 §5).

**OPEN — REQUIRES FURTHER RESEARCH (MLA-009):** *The specific technical implementation of data minimization (retention periods, on-device vs. server-side processing, exact disclosure language for caregivers) is not specified in any document reviewed and belongs to the future Parent Bible and to direct engineering specification, not to this Bible.*
