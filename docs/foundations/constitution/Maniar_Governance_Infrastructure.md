## ۱. منشور دادگاه قانون اساسی (Constitutional Court Charter)

- **Constitutional Role:** The Constitutional Court constitutes the exclusive adjudicative authority responsible for determining constitutional compliance. It exercises no legislative, operational, or configurational authority.
- **Jurisdiction:** Restricted to determining admissibility, standing, scope, proportionality, continuing justification, and termination (APS-3).
- **Limitations:** The Court shall not create authority, amend vocabulary, prescribe implementation, optimize behavior, or substitute technical for constitutional judgment.
- **Standard of Review:** Derived exclusively from APS-1 through APS-4, World Specifications, and formal Constitutional Submissions. No external doctrine possesses constitutional force.
- **Burden of Proof:** Remains exclusively upon the proponent of intervention. The Court neither supplements nor reconstructs deficient arguments.

## ۲. Charter of Constitutional Baseline — CWS-Calm-0000-A

- **Constitutional Declaration:** The constitutional order of the Calm World exists independently of constitutional intervention. Constitutional authority, jurisdiction, and review mechanisms exist before any case is filed. Accordingly, the constitutionally preferred condition of the Calm World is Constitutional Observation.
- **Constitutional Maxim:** "The first duty of constitutional authority is not to intervene, but to preserve the constitutional conditions under which lawful intervention remains possible."
- **Interpretive Note:** The absence of intervention shall never be interpreted as constitutional inactivity. Rather, it constitutes the fullest realization of Constitutional Preservation whenever no constitutionally sufficient grounds for intervention have been established.

## ۳. Interpretive Declaration — CWS-Calm-0000-B

- **Constitutional Purpose:** The Maniar framework exists to preserve the constitutional conditions under which lawful acoustic intervention may occur.
- **Philosophy of Normative Restraint:** Constitutional preservation is sustained through the maintenance of jurisdictional boundaries rather than the continual expansion of constitutional legislation.
- **Constitutional Orientation:** Within the Maniar framework, intervention constitutes an exception requiring constitutional justification. Observation constitutes the ordinary constitutional condition.
