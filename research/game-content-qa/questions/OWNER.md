## Purpose & Scope
This directory stores direct audio recordings and transcript documents containing technical and game-design questions submitted by the game development team regarding environmental interactions, visual feedback limits, and world triggers.

These records serve as the authoritative baseline for queries raised during the design and development of the child-facing interaction worlds.
