## Scope & Purpose
This directory stores the direct questions, technical design dilemmas, recorded audio queries, and visual environment samples submitted by the game engine and content development teams. 

These questions target sensory constraints, interactive boundaries, environmental visual density, pacing, and mechanics suitable for autistic children, forming the inquiry baseline that Dr. Akhgari reviewed and answered.
