## Purpose & Scope
This directory stores visual/environmental reference samples used by the game content team to verify whether candidate game environments adequately convey the meaning and context of specific questions.

These samples were used to review environmental questions (e.g., "Which of these environments does this concept resemble?") by comparing candidate visual representations against alternatives. They map what a question requires the child to perceive and understand to the actual in-game environment representation.

These materials are QA references only. They are not approved final game questions, clinical guidance, or production-ready environment assets unless separately reviewed and explicitly approved.

## Governance & Review Policy
- **Reference Status:** Materials in this folder are visual reference benchmarks for QA assessment only and must not be imported directly into production assets without explicit UX/Clinical sign-off.
- **Audit Trail:** Superseded or rejected visual samples must not be deleted. Mark them as `Deprecated` or `Rejected` to preserve the evaluation history.
- **Review Requirement:** An environment sample may be promoted to production only after the UX and game development teams confirm it meets sensory accessibility standards and accurately reflects the prompt's intent.
- **Clinical Review:** Any environmental element intended to test cognitive or developmental comprehension requires review by the Clinical Research & Advisory Board before release.
- **Privacy:** Do not store children's or families' PHI/PII or confidential clinical data in this directory.
