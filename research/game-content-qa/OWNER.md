## Scope & Purpose
This directory serves as the centralized repository for all technical, artistic, and design queries raised by the game development and content creation teams regarding the interactive environments of **Maniarverse**, alongside the official expert responses and clinical validations provided by Dr. Akhgari.

It bridges technical implementation (3D assets, pacing, sensory thresholds, interaction limits) with evidence-based autism design principles to ensure a non-overstimulating, calming user experience.
