## Core Clinical Directives (Product & Engineering Guidelines)
1. **Terminology Compliance:**
   - **Allowed:** *Self-Regulation*, *Emotion Regulation*, *Arousal Adjustment*, *Observable Engagement*.
   - **Prohibited:** *Nervous System Reset*, *Brain Balancing*, *Therapeutic Treatment*, or medical intervention claims.
2. **Product Positioning:**
   - Framed strictly as a **"Child-Centered Digital Visual Experience"** designed to engage and support sensory-sensitive children without making diagnostic or clinical therapeutic guarantees.
3. **Age Decoupling Policy:**
   - **Product Age Range:** Broad coverage (e.g., 3–7 years).
   - **Research Study Cohorts:** Controlled age-stratified cohorts (3–5 years vs. 5–7 years) for empirical measurement.
